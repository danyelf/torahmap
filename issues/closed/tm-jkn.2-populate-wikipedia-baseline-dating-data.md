# Populate Wikipedia baseline dating data

Extract dating information from Wikipedia 'Dating the Bible' article and populate data/text-dating-source.json.

- Era assignment (early_monarchic, late_monarchic, exilic, persian, hellenistic)

Focus on major compositional layers (e.g., Genesis P vs J/E, Isaiah tripartite, etc.).
