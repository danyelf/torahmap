# Show haftarahs for special occasions

Add support for special occasion haftarot beyond the regular weekly parshiot. This includes:
- Rosh Chodesh (new month)
- The Four Special Shabbatot (Shekalim, Zachor, Parah, HaChodesh)
- High Holidays (Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, etc.)
- Fast days (Tisha B'Av, etc.)
- Festivals (Pesach, Shavuot, etc.)

These haftarot often override or supplement the regular weekly readings and have their own traditional connections.
